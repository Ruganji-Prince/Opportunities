# Transparent relevance and eligibility

Edit `config/profile.yaml` to customize verified skills and separate research interests. The supplied profile describes an MSc AI candidate at CMU Africa, expected graduation May 2027, with a mechanical-engineering degree and an international academic exchange. The CV was reviewed locally on 7 November 2026. `cv_documented_skills` contains skills directly supported by its text; `verified_skills` retains explicit claims in the project brief. Matching uses the union, deduplicated. Experience and project summaries contain only relevant professional evidence. The exact May graduation timing comes from the brief; the CV establishes the year. Citizenship is unknown; Rwanda residence does not establish nationality. None of the expanded deep-learning taxonomy is automatically presented as verified experience.

`config/scoring.yaml` controls the five normalized weights: technical alignment 35, academic eligibility 25, career alignment 20, geographic accessibility 10 and funding 10. Technical evidence counts explicit skill and interest mentions, capped at 100% of that component. Academic uncertainty receives partial credit; hard restrictions receive zero. PhD/fellowship/internship career alignment receives more weight than general employment. Worldwide coverage only receives full geography points when explicitly stated. Unknown funding receives partial practical credit without asserting funding.

Scores are deterministic prioritization signals, never acceptance probabilities. Excellent is 85–100, strong 70–84, possible 55–69. Lower relevance and restricted records are hidden in default recommendations. The minimum score can be changed in the UI. Keyword heuristics can over- or under-rank a listing and need periodic review.

Hard restrictions override similarity: explicit postdoctoral/faculty roles, mandatory completed doctorates, senior leadership titles, extensive experience requirements, and explicit citizenship-only restrictions are marked restricted and capped at 30. Where education completion by the start date, nationality, local work authorization or startup eligibility is not established, the result remains unknown with an explanation. No unrestricted eligibility claim is made from host country alone.

Each card shows verified-profile keyword matches, additional interest matches, the career category, uncertain requirements and missing demonstrated specializations. A keyword mention in a description does not establish that it is a mandatory requirement; `required_skills` and qualifications remain unknown unless verified.

To import a CV later: keep the document in ignored `private/`, review its contents locally, and manually update the sanitized verified skill/education fields in `profile.yaml`. Do not upload the raw document, address, phone number, references or contact details. A fully automated CV parser is intentionally not implemented; review is needed to keep unverified inferred skills out of the public profile.
